About TaxWise KE

TaxWise KE is a salary calculation project designed to help users understand gross salary, statutory deductions, taxable income, PAYE and estimated net salary.

The calculations currently follow the rates and rules used in this learning project. They should be verified against current Kenyan payroll requirements before use for actual payroll decisions.
